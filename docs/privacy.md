# Stag Tune Privacy Policy

**Effective date: August 6, 2026**  
**Last updated: August 6, 2026**

Stag Tune is designed to tune instruments without collecting your personal information. This policy explains how the Stag Tune iOS app handles microphone access, app settings, and purchases.

## Microphone access

Stag Tune requests access to your iPhone's microphone so it can detect the pitch of an instrument while the tuner is in use.

- Audio is analyzed in real time on your device.
- Stag Tune does not record or save microphone audio.
- Stag Tune does not transmit microphone audio to the developer or any third party.
- You can revoke microphone access at any time in iOS Settings.

## Information Stag Tune does not collect

Stag Tune does not collect, store, or transmit:

- Names, email addresses, or other contact information
- Precise or approximate location
- Contacts, photos, or files
- Advertising identifiers
- Analytics or usage history
- Microphone recordings

Stag Tune does not track you across apps or websites and does not include third-party advertising or analytics SDKs.

## App settings

Your tuner preferences, such as reference pitch, tuning tolerance, notation, display direction, and haptic preference, are stored locally in the app's preferences on your device. Stag Tune does not transmit these settings to the developer.

## Stag Pro purchases

Stag Pro is offered as an in-app purchase through Apple's App Store. Apple processes the transaction and payment information under its own terms and privacy policy. Stag Tune receives Apple-provided product and entitlement information only as needed to complete, restore, and recognize your purchase. The developer does not receive your payment-card information.

## Third-party services

The current version of Stag Tune does not use third-party advertising, analytics, or tracking services. App distribution and in-app purchases are provided by Apple and are subject to [Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

## Children's privacy

Stag Tune does not knowingly collect personal information from children or any other users.

## Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated if Stag Tune's features or data practices change. The effective date and last-updated date at the top of this page will identify the current version.

## Contact

For privacy questions, contact the developer through the [Stag Tune GitHub repository](https://github.com/spencer-timkar/stag-tune).
